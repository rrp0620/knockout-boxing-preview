About Knockout Boxing & Fitness

A USA Boxing-certified, locally and family-owned boxing gym in Newark, DE.

Who We Are

We utilize boxing and kickboxing style training to deliver to the client what other fitness establishments cannot. We know getting in shape and staying healthy can be challenging, so we offer a variety of programs and classes to fit every personality. Regardless of where you are in your fitness journey, we'll have something just right for you. Whether your goals include getting in shape, training for professional boxing, or anything in between, Knockout Boxing & Fitness is the place to be! Our classes include:

  • Cardio kickboxing
  • Youth boxing
  • Collegiate and sport conditioning
  • Private training
  • Adult skilled boxing

Why Knockout Boxing & Fitness?

There are many gyms to choose from, but none do it quite like us! From our enthusiastic trainers, to the upscale facility and gym equipment, to the dedication to our clientele, we strive to make sure your experience at Knockout Boxing & Fitness is second to none!

  • Exciting programs with experienced trainers and coaches
  • Flexibility for all types of schedules
  • Competitive pricing with flexible payment options
  • Gym is cleaned and sanitized multiple times daily
  • Military, police, nurse, fire, and college student discounts

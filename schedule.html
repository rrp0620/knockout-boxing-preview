Drop-in class prices

On Bookwhen, single classes are listed at $25.00 each for Cardio Boxing/Kickboxing, Kids Boxing and Teen/Adult Boxing. A 2.9% + $0.30 booking fee applies to bookings with a cost. Bookwhen refund policy: "No refunds upon payment".

Before your first class

"We recommend you show up to class 15 minutes early, so you have enough time to stretch and put on your equipment and sign our waiver." Please wear fitness attire and bring a water bottle. For skilled boxing: fitness attire and shoes required. For kids: all equipment needed for class will be provided at the gym.

Boxing & Kickboxing FAQs

Answers to common questions about classes at Knockout Boxing & Fitness in Newark, DE.

Do I need prior boxing experience to join a class?

Prior experience is not required. Our classes are designed to accommodate individuals of all skill levels, including beginners. Each session is structured to be accessible and beneficial for participants with varying degrees of fitness and boxing knowledge.

Can I try a class before committing to a membership?

We offer the option to book a single drop-in class. This allows prospective members to experience our gym environment and training style before making a decision about membership.

What membership options are available?

We provide several membership options: annual memberships, month-to-month plans, and single class drop-in options.

Are your classes suitable for kids?

Yes! We offer youth boxing programs specifically designed for children aged 5 and above. These classes focus on teaching boxing fundamentals and improving fitness in a safe, enjoyable environment tailored for younger participants.

Can I participate in amateur/professional boxing?

We support amateur and professional boxing aspirations. Our gym hosts local shows twice annually and participates in regional tri-state competitions with our amateur team. For those interested in professional boxing, we provide comprehensive training and support to facilitate this transition, including an intensified training regimen and higher performance expectations.

Why cardio kickboxing?

Our cardio kickboxing program offers a comprehensive fitness experience, combining strength training, muscle toning, and high-intensity cardio. Participants can expect to burn between 500-800 calories per session for women and 800-1,000 for men.

What should I bring to class?

Please wear fitness attire and bring a water bottle. We recommend you show up to class 15 minutes early, so you have enough time to stretch, put on your equipment and sign our waiver.

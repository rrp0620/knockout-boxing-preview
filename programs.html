Cardio Kickboxing Group Classes

Experience the ultimate fitness challenge with our cardio kickboxing group classes at Knockout Boxing & Fitness. Our 45-55 minute sessions are meticulously designed to help you burn between 500-1,000 calories, combining strength training, toning exercises, and intense cardio for maximum fat loss. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or new to fitness, our classes cater to all skill levels.

No prior boxing or kickboxing experience is needed and our expert trainers guide you through every movement. These classes include:

  • 10-minute warm-up
  • Four-minute rounds on heavy bags
  • New combinations every 1-2 minutes
  • Active rest intervals
  • Final circuit plus core work

Classes are offered seven days a week, with drop-ins, monthly memberships, and discounted annual plans available. Gloves and wraps are included with enrollment.

Benefits of Cardio Kickboxing

  • Boost cardiovascular endurance
  • Enhance full-body strength
  • Improve coordination and balance
  • Increase flexibility and agility
  • Burn calories efficiently
  • Reduce stress and improve mental focus
  • Build self-confidence through skill development

What to bring

From the Bookwhen class listing: "Please wear fitness attire and bring a water bottle with you to class." We recommend you show up to class 15 minutes early, so you have enough time to stretch, put on your equipment and sign our waiver.

Youth Skilled Boxing (Ages 5–12)

At Knockout Boxing & Fitness, we're passionate about nurturing the next generation of boxers. Our youth skilled boxing program is tailored for children aged 5 to 12, welcoming both beginners and those with prior experience. We've designed this program to introduce kids to the exciting world of boxing while fostering personal growth, discipline, and confidence.

Our comprehensive program covers:

  • Boxing fundamentals
  • Footwork techniques
  • Pad and bag work
  • Optional ring time
  • Offensive strategies
  • Defensive maneuvers
  • Self-defense skills

Why Youth Boxing?

  • Builds confidence and self-esteem
  • Teaches discipline and respect
  • Improves physical fitness and coordination
  • Provides a positive outlet for energy
  • Develops focus and concentration
  • Offers an alternative to traditional team sports
  • Instills valuable self-defense skills

Our patient, experienced coaches create a supportive environment where your child can thrive. Classes are held four days a week, with month-to-month or annual membership plans. We provide all the necessary equipment, including gloves, wraps, and a mouthpiece.

Adult Skilled Boxing Classes

Elevate your boxing skills with our comprehensive adult skilled boxing program. Whether you're a beginner looking to learn the ropes or an experienced boxer aiming to refine your technique, our program caters to all skill levels. We offer a progressive curriculum that starts with fundamental techniques and advances to complex strategies.

Our program includes:

  • Offensive and defensive tactics
  • Intense conditioning
  • Optional live sparring
  • Fundamentals for beginners
  • Advanced techniques for experienced boxers
  • Instruction from national champions and professional contenders
  • Competitive opportunities
  • Path to professional boxing

For those interested in competing, Knockout Boxing & Fitness hosts local shows twice a year and supports an active amateur team. Options include month-to-month or annual memberships, with no contract required. We provide all the necessary equipment, including gloves, wraps, and a mouthpiece.

Benefits of Adult Skilled Boxing

  • Comprehensive physical workout
  • Stress relief
  • Self-defense skills
  • Improved coordination and reflexes
  • Mental toughness and discipline
  • Opportunity for competition
  • Supportive community

Private Boxing & Fitness Training

We offer private training programs tailored to your individual goals. Whether you're aiming for weight loss, muscle building, toning, conditioning, or honing your boxing skills, our experienced trainers are here to guide you. Our boxing and fitness program includes:

  • 30- or 60-minute training sessions
  • Flexible scheduling options
  • In-person and online training (structured four-week remote training blocks available)
  • Access to cardio and skilled boxing classes
  • Tailored programs for weight loss, muscle building, and boxing skills

From $199/month

Pricing starts at just $199/month for 30-minute sessions or $260/month for 60-minute sessions. Trial sessions are also available. As a private training client, you'll also gain access to our cardio and skilled boxing classes at no extra cost.
